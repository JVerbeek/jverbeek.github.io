About

Hello, I'm Janneke!

I am a PhD student supervised by Tom Heskes and Yuliya Shapovalova at Radboud University in Nijmegen, The Netherlands. I like working on projects involving Gaussian processes, preferably more on the theory side. Further research interests include statistical/probabilistic ML in general, changepoint detection, multi-task/mixed frequency regression.

When not working on machine learning stuff, I spend quite a lot of time practicing karate (2nd dan goju-ryu with the IOGKF, teaching occasionally), bouldering, tinkering with my bike or Linux, and reading. Eventually I might write a little about that on here, too.
