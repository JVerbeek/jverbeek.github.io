Hello, I am Janneke Verbeek, a PhD student supervised by Tom Heskes and Yuliya Shapovalova at Radboud University Nijmegen, The Netherlands. My research currently applies Gaussian process (GP) regression to various problems: I have published a paper using GPs for changepoint detection and am currently working on mixed-frequency regression using GPs. In general I am quite interested in anything probabilistic or statistical machine learning.

When not working on machine learning stuff, I keep fairly busy with goju ryu karate (2nd dan IOGKF), bouldering, tinkering with my bike or Linux, origami, a little gardening, and reading. I intend to write a little about that here as well, eventually.

If you want to get in touch, nice! You can find me on LinkedIn, or reach me at firstname.lastname@ru.nl.
